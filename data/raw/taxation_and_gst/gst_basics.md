# GST Basics for Small Businesses
Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax levied in India on the supply of goods and services.
Businesses whose turnover exceeds Rs. 40 lakhs (Rs. 20 lakhs for special category states) are required to register for GST.
A registered person must file GSTR-1 for outward supplies and GSTR-3B for summary of transactions.
